SINGAPORE ROOTS.
A FEARLESS FUTURE.

Founded in 2024 in Singapore, Lefaucheux Esports is a Mobile Legends: Bang Bang organization built around passion for the game and the ambition to compete.

LFX was formed to give talented Singapore players a chance to show what they can do. Opportunity can be hard to find; our ambition is to help players take that next step in competitive MLBB.

OUR IDENTITY.

Philosophy

A team does not need to start big to achieve something big. Commitment, discipline, consistency and the courage to compete against the best matter. Every tournament, victory and defeat is part of becoming stronger.

Team values

Discipline. Loyalty. Growth. Resilience. Ambition.
The values that guide our team, on and beyond the battlefield.

Playing style

Aggressive. Strategic. Fearless.
Calculated aggression, strong teamwork and adaptability. We encourage decisive plays while maintaining discipline and communication as a team.

Brand character

Fearless, ambitious and hungry to prove ourselves. We want the LFX name to signal a competitive challenge. We don’t just want to participate; we want to be remembered.

COMPETITIVE GOALS.

Our ambitions guide the work ahead. They are goals, with no announced qualification or completion dates.

Short term

Establish a consistent competitive presence in Singapore’s Mobile Legends scene, strengthen our roster and challenge ourselves through major tournaments and qualifiers.

Mid term

Compete at a higher level in Singapore, develop stronger players and team infrastructure, and grow both our Singapore roster and Indonesian Ladies division.

Long term

Build LFX into one of Singapore’s recognized competitive MLBB organizations and earn opportunities to represent our name on the international stage.
